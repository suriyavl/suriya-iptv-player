Privacy Policy for IPTV Player

Last updated: July 21, 2026

IPTV Player is a playlist-based media player. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What the app stores

The app stores the following data locally on your device only, using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults):

  • The playlist (M3U/M3U8) and EPG (XMLTV) URLs you enter
  • The parsed channel list from that playlist
  • Your favorited channels

None of this data is transmitted to us, sold, or shared with any third party. We do not operate a server, and the app does not communicate with any backend of ours.

What the app sends over the network

The app makes network requests only to URLs you explicitly provide:

  • Fetching the playlist and EPG files you link
  • Streaming video/audio from the channel URLs contained in that playlist
  • Loading channel logo images referenced in the playlist

These requests go directly from your device to the servers hosting that content — we are not an intermediary and do not see or log this traffic.

Analytics and tracking

This app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect device identifiers, usage statistics, or any personal information.

Third-party content

Playlists and streams are supplied by you, the user, from sources of your choosing. We do not host, curate, or have any relationship with the operators of those sources, and are not responsible for their content or availability.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.
